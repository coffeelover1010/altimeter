# Altimeter

For adventurers and explorers who want to document the world on their journey.

Take an aircraft-style altimeter on your travels through WoW Forever. Follow the
height of the land as you climb mountains, explore valleys and chart your route.
Over mapped ocean water, the instrument switches to a seabed depth gauge.

- Ground elevation and seabed depth in metres.
- Small, medium and large sizes.
- Movable dial with saved size, position and visibility.
- Help and settings in Options > AddOns > Altimeter.
- Use /alt to toggle the display, or its cross to hide it.
- Terrain coverage across 48 maps, including Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor.

Altimeter measures natural terrain and the depth of the seabed, not your
character's altitude or diving depth. Buildings, bridges and cave floors are
not represented. Unsupported areas show no reading; capital cities hide the dial.
The supplied dataset targets Forever build 1.60.1.70862.
